01 — MY STORY

From Philadelphia roots to public service.

My path has been shaped by education, leadership, advocacy, and service. As a Political Science graduate of Neumann University, I developed an academic foundation in government and public policy while gaining practical experience representing students and working with communities.

Those experiences taught me that leadership is not simply about having a title. It is about listening, communicating, solving problems, bringing people together, and making sure people have a voice at the table.

03 — SERVICE

Service before office.

Public service starts long before a campaign. My experiences have included community service, food-bank and soup-kitchen volunteering, park cleanups, youth engagement, and service-oriented work through my university community.

Through Franciscan Justice Circle work, I also explored poverty, social justice, environmental responsibility, inclusion, and the impact that economic hardship can have on young people.

These experiences strengthened my belief that government should be accessible, responsive, and focused on creating real opportunity.
